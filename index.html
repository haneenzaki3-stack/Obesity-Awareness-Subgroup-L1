01 — THE BASICS

Understanding Obesity

Obesity is a chronic, complex disease characterized by excessive or abnormal body fat that may negatively affect health.

How is obesity assessed?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening tool calculated by dividing a person's weight in kilograms by the square of their height in meters.

Important: BMI does not directly measure body fat and should not be used alone to determine a person's health. For children and teenagers, BMI must be interpreted according to age and sex using appropriate growth charts.

02 — WHY IT HAPPENS

Causes & Risk Factors

Obesity does not have one single cause. It develops through a complex interaction of biological, environmental, social, and behavioral factors.

Genetic Factors

Genetics can influence appetite, metabolism, and how the body stores fat.

Environmental Factors

Food availability, access to safe places for physical activity, and daily surroundings can influence health habits.

Medical & Biological Factors

Certain medical conditions, medications, hormones, and sleep patterns may contribute to weight changes.

Lifestyle Factors

Eating patterns, physical activity, sleep, and daily routines can all play a role.

03 — HEALTH IMPACT

Health Complications

Obesity may increase the risk of several health conditions. The effects can differ from one person to another.

Type 2 Diabetes

Obesity is associated with insulin resistance, which can increase the risk of type 2 diabetes.

Cardiovascular Diseases

It may increase the risk of high blood pressure, abnormal blood lipids, and heart disease.

Joint Problems

Increased mechanical stress and other biological factors may contribute to joint problems, including osteoarthritis.

Sleep Problems

Obesity is associated with a higher risk of obstructive sleep apnea.

04 — TAKING ACTION

Prevention & Management

Obesity management is not about quick fixes. It involves long-term, individualized care and support from healthcare professionals.

Balanced Nutrition

Choosing a variety of nutritious foods and developing sustainable eating habits.

Regular Physical Activity

Including enjoyable movement and physical activity as part of daily life.

Healthy Sleep Habits

Maintaining a regular sleep routine supports overall health.

Medical Support

Healthcare professionals can help assess individual needs and discuss appropriate treatment options.

Every person has different health needs. Treatment and prevention plans should be individualized with professional guidance.
